## Null YT

A YouTube client that looks good, bends to your taste and keeps your feed.

- **Liquid glass or solid.** Every panel can be frosted glass with adjustable blur, tint, rim light and vibrancy, or a clean opaque surface.
- **Your real YouTube feed.** Sign in once, the normal Google way, and get the same recommendations, subscriptions, history, likes and watch later as the official app. No API keys and no Google Cloud project. What you watch here keeps training your recommendations.
- **Customise everything.** Accent colour, font, corner radius, density, layout, navigation, filters, blocklists and player defaults. You can export your whole setup as JSON and share it.
- **Built on [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp),** which is bundled with the app and updates itself.
